ODeL History & Mandate

The founding vision, institutional mandate, and evolution of Open Distance Learning at NSUK.

Institutional Background & Philosophy

Nasarawa State University, Keffi was established in 2001 under the Nasarawa State Law No. 2 of 2001 as passed by the State House of Assembly. The university was founded upon the philosophy of the "belief in excellence, creativity, productivity and freedom of conscience for the advancement of individual and humanity."

The mandate of the university is to pursue and foster "knowledge for development". In line with the vision, thoughts, and aspirations of the founding fathers of the university, the administration seeks to make Nasarawa State University a 21st-century academic giant and pacesetter in teaching, research, and innovation tailored towards people-oriented community service, national development, and global visibility.

The Directorate of Open, Distance and e-Learning (ODeL)

In recognition of the changing dynamics of the global educational ecosystem and the need to democratize higher education for qualified individuals constrained by work, distance, or family commitments, NSUK established the Directorate of Open, Distance and e-Learning with full approval from the National Universities Commission (NUC).

Our Vision

To be a premier e-learning institution in Africa that delivers flexible, inclusive, technology-driven, and internationally recognized academic programs for personal growth and societal transformation.

Our Mission

  • Deliver top-tier tertiary education through innovative digital learning management technologies.
  • Ensure equitable access to quality education regardless of location, background, or physical barriers.
  • Foster lifelong learning, critical inquiry, and technical competency aligned with the contemporary job market.
  • Maintain rigorous academic integrity through cutting-edge Computer-Based Testing (CBT) and proctored assessment systems.

Pioneer Program

The Directorate commenced academic operations with the approved flagship Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Public Administration. Proposed and approved upcoming additions to the Directorate's offerings include Computer Science, Business Administration, Accounting, Entrepreneurship, and Economics.
