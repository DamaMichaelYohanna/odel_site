Admissions & How to Apply

Step-by-step guidance on applying for accredited degree programs at NSUK ODeL.

Application Procedure

4 Easy Steps to Complete Your Application

1

Access the Online Application Portal

Visit the official NSUK Application Portal (ug.nsuk.edu.ng/application) and select the Open, Distance and e-Learning (ODeL) program option.

2

Create Profile & Generate Remita RRR

Enter your active email address, full name, and phone number to create your applicant profile. Generate your unique Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) to pay the application processing fee online or at any commercial bank.

3

Fill Application Form & Upload Credentials

Login to your application dashboard, fill in your bio-data, academic history, O'Level results (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB), and upload scanned copies of your certificates, passport photograph, and direct entry credentials if applicable.

4

Submit Application & Print Acknowledgement

Review your submitted data for accuracy, click submit, and print your formal Application Slip containing your Applicant ID (e.g. ODEL2500008). Keep this ID handy for checking admission updates.
